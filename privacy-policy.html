Frontier Watch Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 30, 2026

Frontier Watch is an offline single-player game published by Sedat Doganer. This policy explains how the game handles information.

Local game data

The game stores campaign progress, settings, earned Stars and Gems, purchased blueprint state, tutorial state, and optional-ad preferences locally on your device. The publisher does not operate an account system or server that receives this save file.

Optional rewarded advertising

Frontier Watch offers optional rewarded ads only after you make a privacy choice in the game. Unity Ads may process device identifiers, IP address, general device and network information, diagnostics, and ad interaction data to deliver, secure, measure, and personalize or limit advertising according to your choice and applicable law. Ads are never required to complete campaign content, and rewards are granted only after a completed ad.

Unity's privacy information is available at unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Children

Frontier Watch is not directed to children under the minimum digital-consent age in their region. The publisher does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Data retention and deletion

Local save data remains on the device until the app data is cleared or the app is removed. For advertising privacy or deletion requests, use Unity's privacy controls presented with an ad or contact us so we can direct you to the applicable provider process.

Changes

This policy may be updated when game features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
