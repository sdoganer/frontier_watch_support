Privacy policy

Effective date: September 11, 2026

Frontier Watch is a single-player game published by Sedat Doganer. This policy covers the iOS game and these player-support pages.

The game has no advertising or game account. Campaign saves stay on your device. Apple and Unity process purchasing information as described below.

Local game data

The game stores progress, Stars, Gems, Guards, blueprint ownership, upgrades, heroes, discoveries, tutorials and settings in local save files and a backup on your device. Casual, Standard and Veteran have separate campaign data. The publisher does not operate a cloud-save service or gameplay server that receives these files.

Purchases and device information

Apple processes the optional permanent full-campaign purchase. The game uses Apple's StoreKit and Unity In-App Purchasing to request prices, process purchases, verify ownership, restore access and check refunds or revocations. The game does not receive your payment-card details.

Unity's purchasing SDK sends purchase events and associated information to Unity. This can include product and transaction identifiers, price and currency, purchase status, signed transaction or receipt information, an app-vendor device identifier, device model, operating system, language and locale, storage capacity and device boot time, installation time, app and SDK versions, and available session or installation identifiers. Unity processes this information for its purchasing services and related analytics. The game does not enable a separate gameplay analytics or crash-reporting service.

A verified transaction identifier is also cached in the device's Keychain, separately from campaign saves, to support offline campaign access. The game's Keychain cache is device-local and is not configured to synchronize through iCloud.

For the providers' processing, retention and privacy choices, see Apple's privacy policy, Unity's privacy policy and Unity's purchasing privacy information.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any details or attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate problems and maintain support records. Please send only what is needed for the request.

Support email is handled through Gmail. Its provider's practices are explained in Google's privacy policy. Support records are retained as needed to resolve requests, maintain necessary records and meet legal obligations. You can contact us to request access, correction or deletion.

Visiting these pages

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels or cookies to them. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security, including when visitors are not signed in. See GitHub Pages data collection and GitHub's privacy statement.

Following an external link opens a service governed by that provider's privacy policy.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Removing the app or clearing its storage can delete local campaign saves. We cannot recover a deleted save through a publisher account. Keychain records may survive reinstall, and Apple maintains its purchase history separately. Restore restores campaign ownership, not deleted game progress.

Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of personal information. Contact us for requests about the game, support messages, or Unity purchasing data processed on our behalf; we will coordinate applicable requests with Unity. For information Apple, Unity, Google or GitHub processes independently, use that provider's privacy contact. Their policies explain their processing locations, retention and privacy rights.

Children and parents

The game has no player accounts, chat or public uploads. Parents or guardians can contact us with privacy concerns and use Apple's device settings to manage in-app purchases.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when features, providers or privacy practices change. The effective date above identifies this version.
