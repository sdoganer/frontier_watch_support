For help, email sedatdoganer@gmail.com.

Include with your report

  • Device model and operating-system version.
  • Game version shown on the store or device settings page.
  • Level, difficulty, and what happened immediately before the issue.
  • A screenshot if it does not contain private information.

Save data

Progress is stored locally. Removing the app or clearing its storage can permanently remove progress. Do not reinstall while troubleshooting unless support specifically recommends it.

Optional rewarded ads

Rewards require a network connection and are limited to three completed rewards per UTC day. Skipped, unavailable, or failed ads do not grant Gems. Campaign progress does not require ads.
